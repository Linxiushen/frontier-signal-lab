Existing caller markers in recognized positions suppress generation; schema and tool-argument keys are not recursively mistaken for cache markers. `EvalClientConfig.cache_retention=None` follows `PI_CACHE_RETENTION` when the helper runs, after trimming/case folding; accepted environment values are `none`, `short` and `long`, otherwise short. Explicit config wins. Short emits an ephemeral marker without TTL; long adds `ttl: "1h"`; none disables generated markers and preserves caller markers. Thus unchanged serialized config alone does not identify the resolved outbound policy when the environment is consulted. This is a source property, not an observed retry failure.
